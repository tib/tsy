When I get stuck, I usually try to think harder. I reread the same lines, open another tab, and hope that one more piece of information will make the answer appear. Sometimes it does. Often I am just adding noise.

A short walk gives the problem a different kind of space. I do not take notes or listen to anything. I let my attention move between the path, the weather, and whatever question is still waiting in the background.

The solution does not always arrive during the walk. More often, I return with a clearer sense of what I do not know, which is enough to choose a useful next experiment.

I am trying to treat breaks as part of thinking rather than a reward for finishing. Some ideas need a little distance before I can see their shape.
