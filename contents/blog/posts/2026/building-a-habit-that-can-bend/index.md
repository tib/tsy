When I start a new habit, I tend to design it for an ideal day. The schedule is clear, the energy is high, and nothing unexpected happens. Real days are rarely so cooperative.

I am learning to make habits flexible enough to survive a change in plans. A longer yoga session can become a few minutes of breathing. A writing session can become a short note about what I want to remember. The smaller version keeps the door open without pretending that every day is the same.

This flexibility helps me return after I miss a day. I do not have to restart from zero or make up for lost time. I can simply begin again at the size that fits today.

A useful habit should support a life, not compete with it. I want routines that can bend when the day asks for something different and still point me toward what matters.
