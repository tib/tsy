I used to treat rest as something that came after the important work was done. The list was rarely finished, so the pause kept moving to a later hour.

Now I am trying to take short breaks before I feel completely drained. I stand up, look away from the screen, stretch, or walk around the block. The work is still there when I return, but my attention has a chance to reset.

This is not a productivity trick. Some days the most useful thing I can do is stop for longer and let the task wait. A body is not a machine that becomes more reliable when I ignore every signal.

Rest belongs inside a sustainable practice. It helps me return with enough patience to notice details, make decisions, and keep caring about the work I am doing.
