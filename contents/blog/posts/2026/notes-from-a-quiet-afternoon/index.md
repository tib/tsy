I left a blank hour in the afternoon and did not know what to do with it. My first instinct was to fill it with an errand or a small task. Instead I made tea and sat near the window while the light changed.

At first, my thoughts kept returning to unfinished work. After a while they became less orderly. I remembered a question from a book, then a design idea, then a conversation I wanted to continue. None of these thoughts needed an immediate action.

It reminded me that creative work is not only the moment when I make something. It also depends on collecting impressions and letting them find their own connections.

An empty hour can feel wasteful when I measure it only by output. Today it felt more like preparation: a little quiet in which the next idea could arrive without being summoned.
