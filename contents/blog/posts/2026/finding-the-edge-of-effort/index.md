In a pose, there is a moment when effort feels steady and useful. There is also a moment just beyond it, where I start holding my breath and bargaining with my body. I am learning to recognize the difference.

The edge is not fixed. It changes with sleep, stress, and the day I have already had. What felt easy yesterday may ask for more attention today. That is not a failure of progress; it is information about the conditions I am practicing in.

I try to bring the same curiosity to work. A challenging task can be engaging, but if I stop taking breaks and begin to resent every interruption, I may have crossed my own edge. The answer is not always to push through.

Finding a sustainable effort takes honest attention. I am still learning to listen before discomfort becomes the loudest voice in the room.
