On busy days, I sometimes assume that if I cannot make time for a full practice, there is no point in starting. That all-or-nothing thinking has kept me away from the mat more than once.

I am experimenting with a shorter transition: stand still, take a few comfortable breaths, roll the shoulders, and fold forward only as far as feels kind. It takes a few minutes. It does not need a playlist or a plan.

The movement itself is simple. What matters is the moment of choosing to notice how I arrived at the end of the day. Am I still carrying tension in my jaw? Is my breathing shallow? What would help me soften by one small degree?

A short practice cannot solve a long day, but it can help me meet the evening as something separate from work.
