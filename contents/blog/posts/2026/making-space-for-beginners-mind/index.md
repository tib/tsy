Being new at something is uncomfortable in a very specific way. I want to understand the shape of a pose before my body has learned how it feels. I compare my first attempt with an imaginary version of what I think progress should look like.

Yoga gives me many chances to notice that habit. The teacher offers a cue, I try it, and the result is simply the information I have for that moment. There is no need to turn it into a judgment about what I can or cannot do.

That beginner's mind is useful outside the studio too. When I learn an unfamiliar part of Swift or explore a new tool, I do better when I ask simple questions instead of pretending I should already know the answer.

I want to keep the curiosity that comes with starting. It makes room for progress that is honest, gradual, and my own.
